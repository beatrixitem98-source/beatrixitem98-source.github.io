Northstar Supply: supplier feed cleanup and pre-import validation

Independent practice project focused on supplier-feed cleanup, exception handling, field mapping, and pre-import validation.

  • 18 source rows
  • 11 READY
  • 7 HOLD
  • 6 exception types

The problem

The supplier feed contained inconsistent formatting, a missing price, negative inventory, a weight supplied in pounds while the staging standard used grams, a malformed image URL, a missing product type, and a duplicate SKU affecting two rows.

What I did

I reviewed 18 supplier rows, mapped source fields into a Shopify/Matrixify-style staging structure, and applied checks for duplicate SKUs, price, inventory, weight presence, HTTPS image format, and product type.

Checks and result

11 rows cleared the staging gate. 7 rows remained on HOLD across 6 documented exception types because the source data still needed correction or approval.

Controlled update check

A controlled workbook validation case deliberately introduced a mismatched Variant SKU, blocked it, restored the approved staging value, and rechecked the baseline before import.
