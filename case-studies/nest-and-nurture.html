Nest & Nurture: Shopify catalogue cleanup, bulk updates, and QA

Independent Shopify practice project. Solo work in Shopify Admin, Matrixify, and Google Sheets.

  • Role: solo
  • Store: 82 products (27 active, 54 draft, 1 archived)
  • Tools: Shopify Admin, Matrixify, Google Sheets
  • Practice store: Open practice store ↗

What this project covers

Nest & Nurture is my independent practice store. I used it to do catalogue work in real Shopify Admin and Matrixify: cleaning product data, running controlled bulk updates, structuring variants and SKUs, organizing collections, and checking the storefront.

Catalogue cleanup and data prep

A practice export with case errors, colour and price in one cell, blank fields, and a duplicate row. I cleaned it in Google Sheets, matched SKUs to variants, and flagged missing barcodes instead of inventing them.

Controlled Matrixify updates

Before a bulk import I decide the match key, check it in Shopify, and test one row. I ran two one-row UPDATE tests on an existing draft product.

Test 1: accepted Variant ID update

25 Sep 2026. Updated one variant by Variant ID. The price was written at its current value (PHP 320), a deliberate no-op, so any other difference would show something else had moved. All 8 checked fields matched Shopify. Decision: accept.

Test 2: deliberately wrong Product ID

26 Sep 2026. A controlled one-row UPDATE used a deliberately wrong Product ID. Matrixify failed the row because the product did not exist. Shopify reconciliation confirmed no product was created and the original Draft product and variant were unchanged.

Variants and SKUs

  • Normalized capitalization and split the combined colour and price cells.
  • Recovered blank titles, SKUs, and prices from the approved product lists, not by guessing.

Collections and organization

Five main collections built from tag and type rules, one manual collection, and one troubleshooting exercise where a product was missing from an automated collection. Checked 24 Sep 2026.

Catalogue QA and alt text

I audited 28 product descriptions against the fictional supplier packet, then checked all 27 active product pages. I audited image alt text, filled the documented blanks, and logged open items instead of guessing.

Storefront validation

A final pass on the customer-facing practice storefront: navigation, neutral collection wording, one corrected care line, and mobile cart/payment-button visibility.

Scope and limits

  • Independent practice store, not paid client work.
  • The Matrixify tests were one row each, on the Matrixify Demo plan. I make no claim about large imports, time saved, or error rates.
  • The care line is approved fictional practice-store supplier wording, not manufacturer verification.
  • No sales, orders, revenue, or customer results are claimed.
