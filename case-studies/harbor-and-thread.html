Harbor & Thread: variant architecture and SKU integrity

Independent practice project focused on variant architecture, SKU integrity, collection logic, and bulk catalogue validation.

  • 3 products
  • 24 variants
  • 24 unique SKUs
  • 24 of 24 final PASS

The problem

The catalogue covers 3 apparel products and 24 size/color variants. The source data included inconsistent option naming, a duplicate SKU, a missing SKU, negative inventory, a missing variant image, and a text-formatted price.

What I did

I normalized size and color values, generated a consistent SKU structure, resolved the documented source issues, checked inventory and image coverage, and organized the catalogue into Tops, Knitwear, and Outerwear with Color, Size, and Material filter values.

The Charcoal/L sweater row contained -2 inventory in the source. The project brief supplied 11 as the documented resolution value, and that reference is recorded in the Source Product Brief sheet. I used the supplied value rather than deriving or guessing a quantity.

Checks and result

I prepared 24 UPDATE rows as a follow-up update set for products assumed to already exist after an earlier approved import. The collection check compared each prepared row with the expected merchandising rule. One controlled QA test case produced a mismatch on Canvas Overshirt / Navy / L; the row was blocked, the collection was corrected to Outerwear, and the typed final value was checked against the expected collection.

The final prepared dataset contains 24 unique SKUs, complete collection/filter coverage, valid inventory and image checks, and consistent counts across Tops, Knitwear, and Outerwear.

Collection counts: 12 Tops, 6 Knitwear, 6 Outerwear. Final validation: 24 of 24 PASS.
