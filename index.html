Questions before we start

What kind of catalogue problems do you work on?

Messy CSV or supplier data, inconsistent SKUs and variants, collections that do not match the products, missing alt text, and bulk updates that need a safe workflow.

How do you avoid creating duplicate products in a bulk update?

I decide the match key first (for example product ID or variant ID), check it in Shopify, run a one-row test, and compare the result with Shopify before anything larger.

How do you QA a catalogue?

I compare the source data with Shopify, then check titles, images, alt text, SKUs, variants, collections, and the storefront page. Anything I cannot verify is logged as an open exception. I do not guess.

Can you work in an existing store?

Yes. I start with an audit and agree the scope before changing anything. Changes are made in controlled steps, and I ask for a backup export before bulk updates.

What do you need from me to start?

Your store URL, the approximate number of products and variants, the catalogue problem, the deliverable you want, and your deadline. Access is agreed before work starts and limited to what the task needs.

How do you protect live store data?

I make a backup export first, test on one row, preview the import, write only the fields the task needs, and check the records again afterward.

Are these client projects?

No. All four are independent practice projects using practice or fictional data. Nest & Nurture is a Shopify practice store where I performed real Shopify Admin and Matrixify work. The three supporting projects demonstrate supplier-data preparation, variant and SKU structure, catalogue taxonomy, and documented QA. Each case study explains its scope and evidence.

How is the scope decided?

After the audit I list the issues found, what I would change, and what I would leave alone, and we agree that before I start.

Need help with your Shopify catalogue?

Send your store URL and a short description of the problem. Written details are fine, and a call is optional.
