Morrow Home: catalogue taxonomy and merchandising

Independent practice project focused on catalogue taxonomy, collections, navigation, product content, SEO fields, and media organization.

  • 12 products
  • 4 collections: 6 / 2 / 2 / 2
  • 5 navigation targets
  • 12 product types normalized

The problem

The Source Brief contained inconsistent product types and room labels, uneven material naming, vague titles, and one duplicate raw handle. My initial navigation draft also had an incorrect Workspace target.

What I did

I normalized 12 product types, assigned all 12 products to four room-based collections, and mapped five navigation targets. I organized product titles, descriptions, unique handles, SEO titles, meta descriptions, planned media filenames, and image-specific alt-text drafts. I documented the Bedroom placement of one curtain as a project merchandising decision because its source room was blank.

The Bedroom placement of MH-010 is a documented merchandising decision, because the source room was blank.

Checks and result

Formula-driven checks confirm the 6 / 2 / 2 / 2 collection split, five final navigation targets, the corrected Workspace path, 12 unique handles, 12 SEO titles, 12 meta descriptions, 24 unique planned filenames, and 24 unique alt-text drafts. SEO character ranges are internal project standards. Editorial accuracy was separately reviewed against the Source Brief and documented merchandising decisions. Alt text must be checked against actual images before publication.

The deliverable is a prepared catalogue and merchandising model for possible later Shopify implementation. It is not a Shopify-native import file. This practice project makes no sales, ranking, conversion, or revenue claims.

The Workspace navigation target was corrected from /collections/bedroom to /collections/workspace in the final navigation model.
